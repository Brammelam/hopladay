How this example is calculated

The balanced strategy uses a defined length and seasonal score: at most 4 breaks, 3–21 days per break, a preferred length of 9 days, and at least 21 days between breaks. No preferred months, company holidays or approved leave were supplied for this example.

The deterministic engine optimizes that declared balanced score under the allowance and separation constraints. This is not a claim that one plan is universally best. It can leave part of the allowance unused rather than force an unsuitable break.

Assumptions and holiday data

This example assumes a Monday–Friday workweek and nationwide public holidays only. Regional holidays, custom work schedules and employer-specific entitlement can change the result. A public holiday does not guarantee a day off for every worker; verify your entitlement with your employer.
